About Greenland Group

Your trusted molded pulp packaging partner since 2009

Founded in 2009 in Hong Kong, Greenland Enterprise Group Co., Ltd. is a leading provider of eco-friendly molded pulp packaging solutions in Southeast Asia. We specialize in serving the footwear, furniture, electrical tools, and home appliance industries with sustainable, high-quality paper-based packaging products.

With a comprehensive one-stop service covering design, mold development, and large-scale production, we are committed to the philosophy of "paper replacing plastic" — driving the global transition toward a greener, more sustainable packaging future.
